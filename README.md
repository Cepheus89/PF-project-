Game: Bullet Requiem

Overview: 
Bullet Requiem is a bullet hell styled game with a boss and 3 phases, built using SFML 3.0. The boss in question is “Mehmood Sahab” and he will have different attacks per phase which the player is meant to overcome
Objective:
Use the given abilities to combat the boss and defeat him

Features:
Fighting Mechanics:
Phase 1: 
The Boss has multiple projectiles which it launches at the player and the player has their own projectiles they launch back at the boss
Phase 2: 
The boss drops us onto a scrolling lava map which the player is meant to navigate through without dying to the lava
Phase 3:
The final phase is based off the boss throwing projectiles at us and us reflecting them back at him
Note: Each of these phases have their own specific songs

Difficulty Settings:
The boss will have 3 different difficulty settings in accordance with the players requirements, from easy to Bullet Hell.

Controls:
WASD for movement, Enter for dialogue navigation and space for reflecting and shooting.

Health Bars:
Both the player and the boss have a health bar. If the health bar for the player depletes, the game is over and you will have to redo from the beginning(save for one specific circumstance which you shall see)
Debug Section:
For a developer to perhaps change the settings according to their likings

Skins:
Six separate skins to choose from. The colours being as follows
Red
Golden
Blue
Green
Pink
purple

Interactive Menu Screen:
Controls for adjusting audio, screen shake and hit sound effect.
